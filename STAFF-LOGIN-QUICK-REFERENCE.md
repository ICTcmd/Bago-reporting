## 🆘 Common Issues

### Can't log in?
- ✅ Check if CAPS LOCK is on
- ✅ Make sure email is spelled correctly
- ✅ Password is case-sensitive
- ✅ Try copying and pasting password

### "Unauthorized" error?
- ✅ Your account might not be activated yet
- ✅ Contact your administrator

### Forgot what you can do?
- ✅ Your role is listed in your profile (top right corner)
- ✅ Ask your supervisor for clarification

---

## 🔒 Security Tips

### DO:
- ✅ Log out when finished
- ✅ Change your password regularly
- ✅ Use a strong password
- ✅ Keep your password private
- ✅ Report suspicious activity

### DON'T:
- ❌ Share your password with anyone
- ❌ Write password on sticky notes
- ❌ Use the same password elsewhere
- ❌ Leave your session unattended
- ❌ Log in on public computers
